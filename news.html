Press service news

Notices, warnings of the season and press releases of Irad Gidromet, newest first.

2026

  • 27 October 2026
    Heating season: air quality

    With the heating season under way, air in Nurzhan is often unhealthy for sensitive groups on still mornings. Check the index on air quality before exercising outdoors.

  • 20 October 2026
    Mountain passes: first snow

    The first snow has fallen on the high passes. Drivers on State Route 1 should check the mountain forecast before setting out.

  • 9 October 2026
    Winter outlook 2026–27

    The Service expects a winter close to normal in the valleys, with snow in the mountains above the average of recent years in December and January. Cold spells of −20 °C and below are likely in Nurzhan in January. The outlook is a…

  • 14 July 2026
    Heat warning for the lower Chu and the Akzhaykyn

    From 15 to 19 July daytime temperatures of 40–43 °C are expected on the lower Chu and in the Akzhaykyn depression. Avoid work in the open between 12:00 and 17:00; give livestock shade and water.

2024

  • 2 December 2024
    Air quality: winter bulletin resumes

    The daily air quality bulletin for Nurzhan, Talas and Iyık Janyl is published every morning at 8:00 until the end of the heating season, and read on IradOne radio.

2023

  • 11 August 2023
    Drought: Toktogul reservoir near minimum

    A dry winter and a hot summer have left the Toktogul reservoir close to its minimum reserve, though above the record low of 2008. River flows in the Naryn basin are 30–40% below normal.

  • 16 January 2022
    Avalanche danger on State Route 1

    After three days of heavy snow the avalanche danger in the Otmek valley is high (4). Three avalanches have reached the road below the tunnel; the Gendarmerie has closed the highway.

  • 26 August 2021
    Low water on the Talas

    In August the flow of the Talas at the border gauge was about half the long-term mean. The Service advises farmers on the canal rotations to expect low water until the autumn rains.

2019

  • 12 November 2019
    PM2.5 monitors in the three cities

    The Service has installed six monitors of fine particles (PM2.5): three in Nurzhan, two in Talas and one in Iyık Janyl. Readings are reported on the US EPA index scale and published from this winter.
