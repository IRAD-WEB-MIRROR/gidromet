Documents and legal acts

The acts under which the Service works, and its manuals and orders that others must follow.

Order of the Director · 11 March 2025 · GM-2025/4

Charges for data, certificates and calibration

A certificate of the weather on a given day: 120 som. Monthly climate tables for one station: 300 som. Calibration of a thermograph: 450 som. Free for schools, the courts and state bodies.

Order of the Director · 19 February 2024 · GM-2024/2

Criteria for warnings of dangerous weather

Heavy snow (20 cm in 12 hours, 10 cm on State Route 1), night frost after 1 May, heat (40 °C and above), wind of 25 m/s, air quality index above 150, avalanche danger 3 and above.

Executive directive · 5 April 2022 · 2022/14

On dry-year water plans for large users

Large water users draw up dry-year plans; the Service's spring flow forecast for the Talas, Chu and Naryn decides when they apply. Limits new rice paddies on the lower Chu.

Order of the Director · 21 October 2019 · GM-2019/6

On the air quality network and the index

Sets up the six PM2.5 monitors, adopts the US EPA index scale and its six colour bands, and fixes the morning bulletin of the heating season.

Act · 26 March 1996 · № 96-09

Hydrometeorological Service Act

Makes the Service the only body that issues official forecasts and warnings of dangerous weather in the Zone; broadcasters and the press must carry its warnings unaltered. As amended in 2008.
