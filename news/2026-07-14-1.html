Heat warning for the lower Chu and the Akzhaykyn

From 15 to 19 July daytime temperatures of 40–43 °C are expected on the lower Chu and in the Akzhaykyn depression. Avoid work in the open between 12:00 and 17:00; give livestock shade and water.
