Winter outlook 2026–27

The Service expects a winter close to normal in the valleys, with snow in the mountains above the average of recent years in December and January. Cold spells of −20 °C and below are likely in Nurzhan in January. The outlook is a guide only and is revised each month.

S. Musaeva, chief forecaster
